# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, hiring managers and engineers evaluating David Xiao, first in a 30-second skim, then through project evidence and experience. Secondary visitors are developers and potential collaborators.

## Product Purpose

A hiring-first portfolio presented as xiaoOS, a plausible fictional operating system. The default arrival opens the Dossier; project, resume and contact documents remain available at their own semantic URLs without the cinematic.

## Brand Personality

Futuristic, creative, hacker. The intended emotional response is confidence in a resourceful engineer's craft, not spectacle. The committed references are ctOS's restrained geometry, Bloomberg Terminal's meaningful density, and a well-designed CLI's clarity.

## Anti-references

Generic identical-card portfolios, gradient text, purple-blue neon, glass everywhere, gamer chrome, fabricated surveillance data, and award-site spectacle hiding substance.

## Design Principles

- Hiring-critical information must survive a quick skim.
- Chrome earns its information; never invent personal or project claims.
- Preserve the recognizable Boot Sequence and continuous city-to-workspace narrative.
- Plain-English navigation and useful detail take priority over fiction.
- The semantic documents are the foundation, not a lesser fallback.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard-operable applications, visible focus, readable contrast, native touch scrolling and always-available exits from the intro. Reduced motion must offer an accessible presentation of the narrative. Assets and animation failure must not gate the documents.
